 # 🌎 Global Superstore Sales Analysis Dashboard – Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Global Superstore Sales Analysis Dashboard** created using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of **sales, profit, cost, orders, customers, markets, categories, and yearly performance**. It helps identify sales trends, profit contribution, and category-wise performance across different markets.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
* Track total profit and total cost.
* Monitor the number of orders and customers.
* Analyze year-over-year sales performance.
* Analyze year-over-year profit performance.
* Compare sales across different product categories.
* Analyze profit contribution by market.
* Understand profit contribution by product category.
* Provide raw data for detailed analysis.
* Create an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. 📅 Year-on-Year Sales

Shows the yearly sales trend from **2011 to 2014** and helps understand the growth in sales over time.

### 2. 💰 Year-on-Year Profit

Displays the yearly profit trend and helps evaluate the improvement in profitability over different years.

### 3. 🛍️ Category-wise Sales

Compares sales across major product categories:

* Technology
* Furniture
* Office Supplies

### 4. 🌎 Profit Contribution by Market

Shows the contribution of different markets to overall profit, including:

* APAC
* EU
* US
* LATAM
* Africa
* EMEA
* Canada

### 5. 📦 Profit Contribution by Category

Visualizes the contribution of different product categories to total profit.

### 6. 📋 Raw Data Table

The dashboard also contains a detailed raw data section with fields such as:

* Order ID
* Customer ID
* Customer Name
* Market
* Country
* City
* State
* Category
* Sub-Category
* Product ID
* Product
